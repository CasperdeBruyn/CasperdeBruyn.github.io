De Grinch sloopt de kerstboom

Its all a game..

To kick off the holiday season, our team was challenged to conceptualize, build, and test a fully playable tabletop game prototype in just a single week.
My Role During this rapid prototyping project, I was heavily involved in the hands-on creation process. I was responsible for crafting the DIY catapults and the physical Christmas tree, as well as laying out the rule sheets and scorecards.
The Concept Inspired by the mechanics of the game "Penguin Panic!", we created a chaotic, interactive physical game called: "De Grinch sloopt de kerstboom" (The Grinch Destroys the Christmas Tree).
Because we only had one week, our focus was entirely on rapid iteration. We built a complete tabletop experience from scratch, including a tiered 3D Christmas tree, DIY popsicle-stick catapults, custom packaging, rule sheets, and scorecards. The gameplay is simple but highly engaging: players use the catapults to shoot at the tree, and the ultimate loser of the game is punished by having to wear a physical Grinch mask!
The absolute highlight of this project was the immediate user testing: seeing people gather around the table, laughing, and actively engaging with our prototype. It proved that a good, fun concept doesn't need months of refinement to bring people together.

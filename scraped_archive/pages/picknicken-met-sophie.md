Picknicken met Sophie
Concept. 2023-2024
For a school project commissioned by a real client, our team developed a visual concept and physical mockup for a cookbook titled "Picknicken met Sophie" (Picnicking with Sophie).
My Role For this project, my role focused on both the conceptual and physical phases. I was responsible for exploring and developing the initial concepts for the line-art illustrations, and I took the lead in crafting the final physical mockup of the book to present to the client.
The Concept The client wanted a clear vision of the book's overall layout and the specific illustration style before proceeding with the full production.
To present our vision, we created a playful, approachable design featuring custom, hand-drawn line-art illustrations and clean, readable typography. Instead of just delivering digital files, we crafted a physical mockup of the cookbook. This gave the client a tangible feel of the final product, showcasing exactly how the ingredients, instructions, and illustrations would seamlessly blend together on the page.

Wat leuk dat je een kijkje neemt in mijn (digitale) chaos!
Zullen we het kort en bondig houden? Dan kun je weer verder met rondneuzen. Zoals je waarschijnlijk al zag, heet ik Casper :] Ik ben in '97 geboren in Delft en studeer momenteel CMD (Communication and Multimedia Design) aan de Hogeschool Utrecht.
In het dagelijks leven duik ik graag in allerlei creatieve uitdagingen. Waar ik voorheen veel met mijn handen werkte in de houtbewerking, elektra en tuinbouw, combineer ik die praktische makers-mindset nu volop met de digitale kant van CMD. Van het ontwerpen van logo's en illustraties tot het bouwen van tastbare installaties—ik knutsel net zo graag achter mijn laptop als in de fysieke wereld. Daarnaast run ik een Discord gaming-community van 150+ leden (en hiervoor zelfs eentje met 500+ leden!), waarvoor ik toffe evenementen organiseer en de visuals maak.
Mijn hobby's? Gezellig gamen met vrienden, muziek luisteren, cultuur snuiven door naar toffe plekken te reizen en nieuwe activiteiten uitproberen om mijn kennis te verbreden. Denk aan het zeefdrukken van een eigen design, posters maken, een ring ontwerpen bij een edelsmid of decoratie maken voor feestjes.
Maar het allerliefste houd ik me bezig met het creëren van immersieve ervaringen. Waarom? Ik vind het fantastisch om installaties te maken of ruimtes aan te kleden om mensen een compleet nieuwe ervaring te geven of een verhaal tot leven te laten komen. Als ik ze even helemaal uit de realiteit kan halen, is mijn missie 100% geslaagd.
Verder ben ik de wereld nog volop aan het ontdekken. Zullen we samen verder reizen? Kijk rustig rond op mijn portfolio en stuur me gerust een berichtje als je vragen hebt of wilt samenwerken!

Welcome to my (digital) chaos, glad you're taking a peek!
Let's keep it short and sweet so you can continue exploring. As you might've guessed already, my name is Casper :] I was born in Delft in '97 and I'm currently studying CMD (Communication and Multimedia Design) at Hogeschool Utrecht.
In daily life, I love diving into all kinds of creative challenges. While my background is deeply rooted in working with my hands—like woodworking, electronics, and horticulture—I now combine that practical maker-mindset with the digital side of CMD. From designing logos and illustrations to building tangible installations, I love crafting just as much behind my laptop as I do in the physical world. On top of that, I run a Discord gaming community of 150+ members (and previously managed one with over 500 members!), where I organize awesome events and design the visuals.
My hobbies? Gaming with friends, listening to music, soaking up culture by traveling to cool places, and trying out new activities to broaden my skills. Think: screen-printing my own designs, creating posters, crafting a ring with a goldsmith, or making decorations for parties!
Most of all, I love creating immersive experiences. Why? I absolutely love building installations or decorating spaces to give people a brand-new experience or bring a story to life. If I can take them completely out of reality for a moment, my mission is 100% accomplished.
I'm still exploring what life has to offer. Shall we continue the journey together? Take a look around my portfolio, and feel free to send me a message if you have any questions or want to collaborate!

2023-2024

De Grinch sloopt de kerstboom
Its all a game..
To kick off the holiday season, our team was challenged to conceptualize, build, and test a fully playable tabletop game prototype in just a single week.
My Role During this rapid prototyping project, I was heavily involved in the hands-on creation process. I was responsible for crafting the DIY catapults and the physical Christmas tree, as well as laying out the rule sheets and scorecards.
The Concept Inspired by the mechanics of the game "Penguin Panic!", we created a chaotic, interactive physical game called: "De Grinch sloopt de kerstboom" (The Grinch Destroys the Christmas Tree).
Because we only had one week, our focus was entirely on rapid iteration. We built a complete tabletop experience from scratch, including a tiered 3D Christmas tree, DIY popsicle-stick catapults, custom packaging, rule sheets, and scorecards. The gameplay is simple but highly engaging: players use the catapults to shoot at the tree, and the ultimate loser of the game is punished by having to wear a physical Grinch mask!
The absolute highlight of this project was the immediate user testing: seeing people gather around the table, laughing, and actively engaging with our prototype. It proved that a good, fun concept doesn't need months of refinement to bring people together.

Grijpmachine
DIY. 2023-2024
For a DIY school project, I designed and built a fully functional physical claw machine, the "Lucky Grapple"—to distribute bags of chips during a school exhibition.
My Role I was responsible for the complete physical construction of this installation. This involved woodworking to build the arcade cabinet, designing and cutting the mechanical parts for the claw, and engineering a custom control system using syringes to operate the machine's movements.
The Concept The goal was to transform a simple task, handing out snacks at an expo, into a playful and engaging experience. Instead of simply handing out bags of chips to visitors, they had to actively operate the custom syringe-controls to play the game and grab their own snack.
This installation is a perfect reflection of my passion for Immersive Design: combining hands-on physical craftsmanship with fun, interactive user experiences.

Hu Begeleidt
Concept. 2022-2023
Commissioned by Hogeschool Utrecht, our team was tasked with increasing awareness for "HU Begeleidt" (the university's student guidance and support service) among the student body.
My Role For this project, I was responsible for designing the campaign's mascot, creating the visual assets for the mood tracker smileys, and developing the functional voting system behind the interactive screens.
The Concept To make the support services more visible and approachable, we designed an interactive campaign. We placed physical "happiness meters" (mood trackers) in the school hallways, supported by informative posters and visual nudges, like colorful footprints on the floor, to guide students toward the interactive touchpoints.
The most impactful part of our concept was the data loop: the input collected from students via the happiness meters was updated live on a digital dashboard in the teachers' lounge. This provided the faculty with real-time insights into the overall well-being and stress levels of the student body, effectively bridging the gap between students and the support staff.

Kodak
Concept. 2023-2024
The Project For a school assignment, I was tasked with creating a conceptual rebranding campaign for an iconic brand. I chose to focus on Kodak.
My Role I was responsible for the complete visual and conceptual design of this campaign, including designing the posters, developing the retro-inspired visual identity, and writing the core slogans.
The Concept The core message of this rebranding campaign is clear: we need to shoot more analog and rely less on our digital smartphones. In a world where people constantly experience life through a screen, this concept encourages everyone to put their digital devices away.
Through bold, retro-style typography and contrasting illustrations (the classic analog camera versus the smartphone), the posters promote a "Screenfree" lifestyle. It’s a call to embrace the authentic, tangible experience of analog photography and to truly live in the moment, rather than just looking at it through a phone screen.

Picknicken met Sophie
Concept. 2023-2024
For a school project commissioned by a real client, our team developed a visual concept and physical mockup for a cookbook titled "Picknicken met Sophie" (Picnicking with Sophie).
My Role For this project, my role focused on both the conceptual and physical phases. I was responsible for exploring and developing the initial concepts for the line-art illustrations, and I took the lead in crafting the final physical mockup of the book to present to the client.

This gave the client a tangible feel of the final product, showcasing exactly how the ingredients, instructions, and illustrations would seamlessly blend together on the page.

Concept. 2023-2024
The Project For the organization "Samen Spelen" (Playing Together), our team designed a braille-based concept game for use in public playgrounds.
My Role For this project, I was responsible for designing the physical game pieces and the game board, researching braille typography, and shaping the overall user interaction.
The Concept The main challenge given to us was to spark conversations between parents at the playground, specifically to raise awareness that the playground is fully accessible for children with disabilities.
Our solution was an interactive braille game. Through this game, children can learn a new skill in a fun, informative way while actively involving their parents. This natural parent-child engagement acts as an icebreaker, making it much easier for parents to connect and start talking with one another.

Concept. 2022-2023
The Project Inspired by a recent article from the investigative journalism platform Follow the Money regarding the shifting housing market, our team developed a conceptual guerrilla campaign to raise awareness about the absurdly high housing prices.
My Role For this campaign, I was responsible for conceptualizing the core message, illustrating the satirical line-art, and executing the physical guerrilla marketing in the city.
The Concept We created a satirical campaign titled "#DANMAARHIER" (If I can't afford a house, I'll just live here!). We designed classic real estate "For Sale" posters featuring humorous line-art and physically placed them in absurd public locations, such as a tree, a bus shelter, and a public trash bin.
By advertising these unlivable spots as potential real estate, like labeling a trash can as a "Room with a jacuzzi", we used physical guerrilla marketing in the real world to make a bold statement. The physical signs, combined with a QR code, were designed to make people stop, laugh, and ultimately reflect on the harsh reality of the ongoing housing crisis.

Vechtclub XL
Concept. 2023-2024
For a school project, our team was commissioned by Vechtclub XL to develop a concept that would bring their community of creative entrepreneurs closer together.
My Role For this project, I was responsible for conducting the initial user research, designing the user interface (UI) for the interactive map, and conceptualizing the community solutions.
The Concept We started our process by talking directly to the community to find out what they were missing. The insights were clear: tenants barely knew who else was working in the building, and standard networking events didn't appeal to them at all.
To solve this visibility issue, we designed a functional, interactive digital floorplan. Through this clickable screen, tenants and visitors can easily search, navigate, and discover exactly who is located where within the building.
Alongside the digital interface, we also advised Vechtclub XL to implement a physical "swap spot" (ruilhok). Our research showed that creating a low-threshold space for sharing and trading physical items naturally fosters organic interactions, building a much stronger and more authentic sense of community than forced networking events.

Street Fresh
Concept. 2023-2024
The Project During a project week, our team was tasked with building a concept for a new startup business from the ground up.
My Role For this project, my role was a mix of physical crafting and strategic design. I was responsible for screen-printing the physical T-shirts, creating the graphic design for one of the shirts, and helping to shape the overall business model.
The Concept We developed a subscription service for uniquely designed T-shirts. Every month, a brand-new, exclusive design is created and presented to our subscribers before any printing takes place.
The customer always has the freedom to opt in for that month's drop or simply skip it. By using this on-demand, user-choice approach, we actively combat unnecessary overproduction and completely eliminate dead stock.

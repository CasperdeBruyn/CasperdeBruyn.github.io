# Casper de Bruyn - OSRS Kerst
URL: https://casperdebruyn.myportfolio.com/osrs-kerstposter

Casper de Bruyn - OSRS Kerst
The Project A festive, custom-designed Christmas poster created to celebrate the holidays with my Old School RuneScape (OSRS) gaming clan.
My Role I was entirely responsible for the visual design and composition of this project. This included recreating the nostalgic, low-poly aesthetic, setting up the wintery scenes, and designing the final layout for our community announcements.
The Concept Managing a gaming community of over 500 members is all about bringing people together, and I wanted to do something special for our members during the holiday season. The goal was to create a piece of promotional art that perfectly captured the iconic, retro art style of OSRS, but with a cozy Christmas twist.
By adding playful details, like the classic ducks wearing Santa hats and a festive winter landscape, the poster added an extra layer of immersion and holiday spirit to our digital space. It is a great example of how I love to use my design skills to build, engage, and entertain digital communities.
